Full-card golden templates deliver biggest impact on consultant — structural-fidelity and layout-variety both went from FAIL to PASS. 40 golden templates give enough coverage for every card to match.

30% generation speedup — single LLM call per card (unified resolver) vs two calls (structure+resolve). Consultant: 142s vs ~200s.

100% template match rate across all three guides — every card found a matching golden template.
