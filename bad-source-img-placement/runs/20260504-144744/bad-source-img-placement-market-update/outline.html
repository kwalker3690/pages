    Why Horizontal SaaS Is Getting Hit Hardest

  • Vertical SaaS (+3%): Owns irreplaceable moats — proprietary industry data, compliance workflows, and years of process history. AI can augment but cannot displace without re-acquiring that data. Switching cost is existential, not cosmetic.
  • Infrastructure (+2%): AI is a direct tailwind. New AI workloads run on Snowflake, Datadog, MongoDB, Cloudflare. More AI deployment = more compute, data, and observability spend.
  • Horizontal SaaS (-35%): Accidentally optimized for replaceability — designed to serve every industry equally, integrating deeply with none. Without proprietary data or long process history, switching is easier. These products track who does what and when — exactly what AI handles well.
  • The real concern is terminal value, not near-term earnings: 85–95% of SaaS enterprise value comes from terminal value. A strong quarter moves <5% of value.     What This Means for Your Company Right Now

  • Prove your moat is real: Investors and CIOs are asking the same question — does your product own irreplaceable data, workflow, or compliance? If not, articulate your switching cost clearly.
  • Rethink pricing before your customers do: Usage/outcome-based models are winning. 35% of CIOs expect flat subscriptions to decline. Get ahead of this shift proactively.
  • AI is a feature AND a threat: 58% of CIOs cite AI additions as the top driver of software spend increases — but 45% of AI budgets are replacing existing software. You need to be on the right side of both trends.
  • Fundraising windows are compressing: Rounds are closing faster, valuations are up sharply, but capital is concentrating in fewer deals. If you're raising, move with urgency — the best terms go to the fastest movers.
  • 2026–2027 is the historical sweet spot: Every major platform shift (Internet, Mobile, Cloud) produced its biggest winners from companies founded 4–6 years in. We are in that window now for AI.
