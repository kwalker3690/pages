Series A climate tech deals drop 40% in 2024. Average time from prototype to commercial scale: 8-12 years. Hardware startups need $50-100M before revenue, far exceeding typical VC appetite.

Regulatory Fragmentation

Carbon credit standards vary across 15+ certification bodies. EU's CBAM affects $150B in trade, but implementation differs by member state. US state-level policies create 50 different compliance frameworks.
