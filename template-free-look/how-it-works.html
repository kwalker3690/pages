Your guide (COMPOSITION.md) is appended whole to all three. It doesn’t reach the planner, the checker, or the fixer.

How this differs from main

Main today
  • A library template is picked in the background while you read the plan.
  • The writer adapts that template’s example slides.
  • Look, type sizes, and decorations come with the template.
  • ~$0.32 · plan in ~24 s · ~97 s after Generate · about 1 in 9 slides sent to the fixer.
Template-free (current)
  • No template: steps 4 and 6 design a look for this deck.
  • The writer invents every layout, guided by the boards and slide plan.
  • Your guide shapes the look, the theme, and every slide.
  • ~$0.52 · plan in ~27 s · ~149 s after Generate · about 1 in 3 slides sent to the fixer.

What changed since last night’s version

  • The planner’s visual-direction turn is gone. The plan shows ~18 s sooner, the theme starts as soon as the boards are painted, and asking to change the look before Generate repaints the boards.
  • The look and theme steps have their own instructions, instead of borrowing the planner’s.
  • The theme step reads type sizes from the boards (with a ceiling for reading and heading text) instead of fixed ranges, and can now set chart style, gradients, shape borders and shadows, spacing, and page numbers.
  • The writer now gets the look notes. Before, notes never reached it on any route.
  • Readability: text is never set in “the slide background color” (which caused a blank slide); strong dot or grid patterns behind text are refused, faint ones allowed; main’s own contrast check now does the judging, with automatic fixes for labels, colored bars, and panel text so the step rarely needs a second try.

Last night’s fixes still apply: template-like type limits, no paper texture on dark panels, readable label and caption colors, panel text color reaching its text.
